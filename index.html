I like to build things
around what I see.

My projects and writing usually start with something I accidentally discovered and thought was cool. And I think that if I can find a way to display it, maybe other people can appreciate it too.

I work mostly with Python to analyze data, and HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to put what I find online. Sometimes that means games I’ve made for my job. Sometimes it’s just a reason to try something new.
